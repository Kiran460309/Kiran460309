## 👋 About

I'm a UX/UI designer and full-stack engineer based in Namakkal, Tamil Nadu.

My journey started in **2024** as a UX/UI designer — obsessing over pixels, flows, and interfaces that just feel right. By **2026**, I went deeper and picked up full-stack engineering — not just "frontend + backend + database," but everything underneath that actually makes a product work in the real world.

Over **1 year of hands-on experience** later, I completed my internship at **Esperia Studio**, where I split my time between shipping AI-powered product features and refining the interfaces around them.

## 🧱 What "Full Stack" Actually Means to Me

Most people stop at frontend and backend. I wanted to understand what's underneath — so I'm going deep into:

`Frontend (UI/UX)` `Database Design & Storage` `Authentication & Authorization` `Hosting & Deployment` `Cloud Infrastructure` `Rate Limiting & API Protection` `Caching (Redis, CDN)` `Load Balancing & Scalability` `Monitoring, Logging & Error Tracking` `Backups, Reliability & Disaster Recovery` `Testing (Unit, Integration, E2E)` `Documentation & Maintenance`

Still a work in progress — I'm a self-learner who'd rather understand the **why** than just ship the **what**.
